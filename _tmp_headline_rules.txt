.5  → bullish
diff > 0.5  → bullish_lean
diff < -1.5 → bearish
diff < -0.5 → bearish_lean
else        → neutral
```

The system is interrogatable, not a black box. Use it when synthesis doesn't match your read.

### What queries DON'T do

- They don't change anything. Read-only. Mutations go through meeting files, brain refinements, or j6_action.json - never queries.
- They don't trigger fresh ingestion. They read from the cache populated by the most recent sweep. For fresher data, use `force_sweep` action (existing query).
- They don't reach source bots. They only see what's been written to `signal_contracts/` and ingested. If a source bot is silent (parked, OAuth dead, etc), queries can't fix that - escalate to Lano.
