Track Record --
How Jordan's directional calls have actually graded out. Each call is scored on its 12-hour window against the market-cap-weighted big-3 move (BTC + ETH + SOL, weighted by live cap share) — a top is a market event, not a BTC one, so his read is graded on the whole market, not one coin. When the big-3 decouple, each call is tagged by structure (together / btc-leading / alts-leading / fracturing) — a fracturing tape is the correlation breaking that precedes a blow-off. Up / Up (micro) / Flat / Down (micro) / Down — a micro-lean is graded against a micro threshold (±0.1%), so a small move you called is a hit, not a miss. FLAT now means genuine stillness, not "no opinion." Partial = half credit. The receipts, not just the live call.

Each call owns ONE window: from the moment it's stamped to 12h later — the clock starts when he answers, not on a fixed grid. One revision per window: pulling that lever grades the live call instantly on however long it actually ran, and the revision then rides the REMAINDER of that same window before grading. The next stamp after that opens a clean 12h. It grades once against the real move, and then it's over — nothing re-grades a stance he didn't re-commit. Partial = half credit. A window that closes with no fresh call is logged as a no-call and counts toward coverage, never toward the rate: not calling is a choice, not a miss. Revision economics: one bullet per window. Spending it LOCKS an interim grade on the live call immediately — that grade is on the record whatever happens next — and the replacement rides only the remainder of that same window. Reaffirming costs the same bullet as flipping. There is no silent edit: every change of mind is a graded event.
